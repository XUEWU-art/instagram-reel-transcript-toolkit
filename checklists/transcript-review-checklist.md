# Short-Video Transcript Review Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing a transcript or WebVTT caption file.

## Source and scope

- Confirm that the source is the intended public video or an authorized local file.
- Confirm that the transcript covers the complete spoken section.
- Keep notes about inaudible or uncertain speech instead of silently inventing words.

## Text review

- Correct obvious names, product terms, and acronyms against the source.
- Preserve the speaker's meaning while adding readable punctuation.
- Remove accidental repeated phrases introduced by transcription.
- Mark meaningful non-speech audio only when it helps the reader.
- Keep paragraph breaks aligned with topic or speaker changes.

## WebVTT review

- Start the file with the `WEBVTT` header.
- Give every cue a start time, an end time, and non-empty text.
- Confirm that each cue ends after it starts.
- Check that adjacent cues do not overlap unintentionally.
- Keep each caption readable without splitting names or short phrases awkwardly.
- Play the media with captions enabled and spot-check synchronization at the beginning, middle, and end.

## Delivery

- Open the TXT file in a plain-text editor and verify its character encoding.
- Open the VTT file in a compatible player or caption editor.
- Remove private information that should not appear in a public transcript.
- Record any known limitations for the person receiving the transcript.
